Diri Insight is a static self-reflection tool in Indonesian. It maps eight dimensions: early safety, self-awareness, emotion regulation, empathy, communication and assertiveness, mindset, boundaries, and resilience. The result is a radar map with a written narrative, not a single score or label.

There are two depths. The full version has 70 questions across nine sections and takes 15–25 minutes; the short version uses 27 core questions and takes 5–8 minutes. Progress carries over, so someone who starts with the short version can continue to the full one without losing answers. The scoring also flags recurring patterns such as people-pleasing, rumination, conflict avoidance, and self-criticism.

Everything runs in the browser. There is no account, backend, or tracking, and answers stay in localStorage on the device. Results can be exported as a PNG summary card, a text report, or a print-friendly page. Keyboard navigation, screen-reader announcements, and light and dark themes are part of the interface.
