Farmers rarely control the price they get for their harvest, but they can often choose when to sell. The dashboard charts historical producer prices from SISKAPERBAPO East Java, so the yearly pattern is visible before they decide when to sell.

The dashboard filters by commodity, market, and period, and exports what is on screen as PNG, CSV, or a short summary for WhatsApp. A planting-time calculator and an analysis panel point out the months that usually bring the highest and lowest prices.

The project is free and still under development. Feedback from farmers and anyone who follows local agricultural markets is welcome.
