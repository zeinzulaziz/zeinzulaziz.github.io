Food Prices

A free dashboard that turns East Java producer price data into a seasonal view farmers can plan around.

HTMLPublic repositoryOct 8, 2026
dashboardagriculture
Open live demoRepository

Farmers rarely control the price they get for their harvest, but they can often choose when to sell. Food Prices charts historical producer prices from SISKAPERBAPO East Java, so the yearly pattern is visible before the decision has to be made.

The dashboard filters by commodity, market, and period, and exports what is on screen as PNG, CSV, or a short summary for WhatsApp. A planting-time calculator and an analysis panel point out the months that usually bring the highest and lowest prices.

The project is free and still under development. Feedback from farmers and anyone who follows local agricultural markets is welcome.
