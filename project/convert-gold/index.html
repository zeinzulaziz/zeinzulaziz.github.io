Convert Gold

A gold price calculator that turns USD per troy ounce into rupiah per gram, with entry, target, and stop-loss numbers.

HTMLPublic repository
calculatorgold
Open live demoRepository

Convert Gold starts from the gold price in USD per troy ounce and the USD/IDR rate, then shows what one gram costs in rupiah. It also lines up the per-gram prices published by Antam, UBS, and Pegadaian, so the gap between spot and retail is visible.

A trader mode adds position planning: entry price, target, stop loss, and the capital you are working with in grams. It returns profit and loss per gram, potential profit for the position, and the risk/reward ratio.

Prices come from a live gold spot feed and the ECB reference rate for USD/IDR, refreshed every few minutes. The whole calculator is one static page.
